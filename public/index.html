How it runs

  1. Join with a nickname and the last 4 characters of your Student ID.
  2. Wait in the lobby until your instructor releases a question.
  3. A two-second ready screen appears, then the timer starts.
  4. Answer once. Faster correct answers score more.

Scoring

A correct answer is worth 500 points, plus up to 500 more depending on how quickly you answer. An incorrect answer scores nothing, and you may only answer once.
